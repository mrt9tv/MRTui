# Mokka Racing Team: Complete Color Palette & iRacing Design Strategy

## Orange Color Analysis: #FF8000 vs #FF6B35

### Color Performance Comparison
**#FF8000 (Pure Orange)**
- **Brightness**: Higher luminance, more visible from distance
- **Contrast with Teal**: Superior contrast ratio for readability
- **iRacing Rendering**: Renders more consistently across lighting conditions
- **Racing Heritage**: Classic motorsport orange (similar to McLaren, but brighter)
- **Energy Level**: More energetic, matches "zoomies" personality

**#FF6B35 (Red-Orange)**
- **Sophistication**: More refined, slightly warmer tone
- **Contrast with Teal**: Good but slightly less pronounced
- **iRacing Rendering**: May appear muted in certain track lighting
- **Professional Appeal**: More "premium" automotive feel
- **Balance**: Calmer energy, more traditional racing approach

### **Recommendation: #FF8000**
For iRacing specifically, **#FF8000 wins** because:
- Superior visibility during online racing (crucial for recognition)
- Better rendering under iRacing's dynamic lighting system
- More distinctive in broadcast/streaming situations
- Perfect energy match for Mokka's enthusiastic personality

## iRacing-Specific Optimization

### Lighting Condition Performance
**Day Racing** (Optimal visibility)
- Teal maintains richness in bright sunlight
- Orange #FF8000 stays vibrant, doesn't wash out
- White elements provide crisp contrast

**Night Racing** (Critical visibility)
- Orange becomes more prominent under artificial lighting
- Teal remains distinctive against dark backgrounds
- Sponsor text readability enhanced by color contrast

**Weather Conditions**
- Rain: Both colors maintain visibility in reflective conditions
- Overcast: Orange prevents livery from appearing dull
- Fog: High contrast ensures recognition at distance

### Broadcasting/Streaming Optimization
**Camera Angles**
- Side Profile: Maximum sponsor visibility with flowing design
- Rear View: Orange wing elements frame sponsor content
- Onboard: Consistent branding visible in cockpit reflections
- Aerial: Roof design elements ensure brand recognition

**Stream Quality**
- Colors chosen for optimal compression (avoid color bleeding)
- High contrast ensures readability at lower resolutions
- Text placement optimized for streaming overlays
